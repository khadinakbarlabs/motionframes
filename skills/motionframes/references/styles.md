# Styles

| style | rig | look |
|---|---|---|
| **ink cartoon (2.5D)** | `bean` | warm gradients, 6-10 px ink outlines, 3 depth layers, soft shadow ellipses, one hot accent |
| **stickman explainer** | `stick` | flat paper background, big white heads, thick ink limbs, one accent colour per idea, hand-drawn boil |
| **line art** | `stick` with `shirt="none"` | white strokes on near-black, open-stroke limbs, nothing filled, glow-free |
| **minimal shapes** | none | geometric props only, bold type in the scene, captions off or `case: "lower"` |

Palette: 1 background family + ink + 1 hot colour (captions.hot words and the key prop share it).
Keep the same character colours across every scene of a short.
